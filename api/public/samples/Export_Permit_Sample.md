## EXPORT CONDITIONS

This permit is issued subject to the following conditions:

1. **Quantity Limit:** Export shall not exceed the authorized quantity
2. **Quality Standards:** Coffee must match the inspected sample
3. **Shipping Timeline:** Must be shipped within permit validity period
4. **Documentation:** All required export documents must be presented
5. **Customs Compliance:** Full compliance with Ethiopian customs regulations
6. **Repatriation:** Foreign exchange must be repatriated per NBE regulations
7. **Origin Integrity:** Coffee must be 100% Ethiopian origin as certified
8. **No Substitution:** Substitution of goods after inspection is prohibited
