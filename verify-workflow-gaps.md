## SUMMARY OF POTENTIAL GAPS

### HIGH PRIORITY (Likely Missing):
1. **Pre-shipment inspection** (SGS/Bureau Veritas) - Common for high-value exports
2. **Sample approval by buyer** - Quality pre-approval before shipment
3. **Border crossing documentation** - Ethiopia to Djibouti transit
4. **Djibouti transit clearance** - Required for crossing through Djibouti
5. **Shipping instruction** - Formal instruction to carrier
6. **Document courier/forwarding** - Physical/electronic document transmission
7. **Discrepancy handling** - What if documents don't match LC?
8. **Chamber of Commerce certification** - Certificate of Origin authentication
9. **Export proceeds repatriation confirmation** - NBE requirement
10. **Destination customs clearance tracking** - For complete traceability

### MEDIUM PRIORITY (Process Dependent):
1. Coffee processing steps (if not already processed)
2. Weight verification before stuffing
3. Bag marking & labeling
4. Port handling charges
5. Terminal receipt
6. Container inspection at port
7. Freight payment confirmation
8. Nostro reconciliation
9. Tax withholding
10. Quality claim period

### LOW PRIORITY (Optional/Rare):
1. Contract amendment
2. Buyer credit verification
3. Packaging material approval
4. Embassy legalization
5. Export subsidy claim
